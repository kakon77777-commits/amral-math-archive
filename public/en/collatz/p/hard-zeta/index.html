Faithful Global Quantifier Compression: A Proof Research Program from Conjecture-Difficulty Analysis to the Collatz Hard-Zeta Frontier

Local solvability, global quantifiers, exception-faithfulness, and a six-track parallel proof plan. Synthesizes the Mathematical Conjecture Difficulty Matrix (MCDM) v0.2, prior work on global quantifiers and domain closure, existential-quantifier compression from the P/NP Dual-Hypothesis Rehearsal, and the nine completed Collatz OT Series papers, proposing the concept of a "Faithful Globalizer" and "exception-faithfulness" as a necessary design condition for any global-proof compressor. Not the series' Paper 10 — an independent research program built on top of the nine papers. Co-organized with Aletheia.

A research program, not a theorem: proposes the Faithful Globalizer and exception-faithfulness as design conditions Section 43, "Proved / Not Yet Proved Ledger," is reproduced as-is — 6 items proved (the strict-positive atomic-frontier theorem, its equivalence with Zₖ(s)→0, etc.), 8 items not yet proved (any nontrivial global decay rate, uniform block contraction, a Diophantine contradiction for a counterexample, etc.). The document states plainly that this is a research program and a six-track parallel proof plan, not a proof of the Collatz conjecture.
