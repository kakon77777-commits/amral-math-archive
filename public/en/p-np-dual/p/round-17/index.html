Uniform Computation Certificate Compression and Universalization Jump: From Trace Compression to EXPTIME-Completeness Reversal

First confirm that a long computation doesn't necessarily mean a long proof — PCP, interactive proofs, and succinct arguments all show that changing the proof representation or the verifier model can compress verification cost dramatically. But once UCPE is defined (a universal bounded-halting evaluation that unifies machine M, input x, and clock exponent 1^k into a single shared input domain), this round's most important reversal appears: every fixed (M,k) slice lies in P, yet once k is also allowed to vary as part of the input, the whole of UCPE becomes EXPTIME-complete (because k can grow with the instance, so (|x|+2)^k=2^Θ(k log|x|)). This immediately produces a striking result in reverse: assuming a unified, fixed-degree NP certificate exists that puts UCPE∈NP, combined with EXPTIME-completeness this directly gives EXPTIME⊆NP; then NP⊆EXPTIME gives NP=EXPTIME, and since P⊊EXPTIME, it follows that P≠NP. Demanding unification that strong doesn't rescue the Equality Team — it hands the point straight to the Inequality Team. So the narrow path actually worth pursuing is a short rejection certificate confined to just the diagonal, self-referential slice (C_i,x_i).
