Round 7 v0.7 2026-07-26

Mixed Polyline–Curvature-Arc Families, Curvature Concentration, and the Value of Discrete Kinks

Compares piecewise-curvature wings (2/3/4/6/8 segments per side) against continuous constant-curvature circular-arc wings, testing whether the near-parallel two-sided wings of Rounds 3–6 are really a discrete-kink skeleton, or just a coarse discrete approximation of a smooth low-curvature wing. Final ranking: discrete-event five-link > constant-curvature arc wing > this round's best multi-segment candidate — spreading finite turning out continuously does release a small amount of congruent-containment pressure.

Exploratory numerical research; not a formal proof; not an interval certificate — Stage status as stated by the package's own documents, reproduced verbatim. Continues Moser Skew Lab v0.6. Container: the Wetzel triangle.
