Round 12 v0.12 2026-07-27

Independent mpmath.iv Replay, Interval Newton, and the Arb-Absent Boundary

Round 11's handoff called for recomputing all interval boxes using Arb/python-flint ball arithmetic, but this round's environment has no python-flint, Arb, or Sage, and no installable version was available from the package repositories. So directed-interval arithmetic via mpmath.iv is used as an independent fallback verification instead, without claiming to have completed an Arb replay. Both of the most critical differences replay successfully and independently, and are strictly positive: s₁₂₀-s₂₇₀∈[1.585e-9,1.692e-9], smooth candidate minus event control ∈[on the order of 1.058e-5]. All 19 closed-form boundaries are confirmed (19/19), and all 12 stationary-point root boxes pass interval Newton (12/12). But of the 18 internal boundaries, only 5 can be directly signed by mpmath.iv; for the rest, natural interval expansion near atanh(±1) (dependency inflation) is too wide to independently reproduce Round 11's narrow boxes — this is not a counterexample, Round 11's closed-form error bounds still stand, they simply were not independently replayed by this round's fallback library. The 579 derivative leaf boxes originally planned for one-by-one replay also could not be completed due to runtime limits. This round explicitly records “not completed” rather than writing it up as “passed.”

Partial independent interval replay; not an Arb certificate; not a formal proof — A stage-status self-declaration from the package's own documents, reproduced as-is.
