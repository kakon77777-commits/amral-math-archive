Round 3 v0.3 2026-07-26

Contact-Ledger Reverse Generation, Mirror-Symmetric Multi-Link Curves, and a Degrees-of-Freedom Validity Test

Rather than generating curves randomly from arbitrary coordinates, the search space is now constrained in reverse, starting from a target contact ledger, with mirror symmetry enforced to eliminate chirality false alarms at the parameter level. The five-link curve outperforms the three-link curve, but the seven-link curve does not outperform the five-link curve — the currently effective degrees of freedom are not the number of segments.

Exploratory numerical research; not a formal proof; not a universal covering certificate — Stage status as self-declared by the in-package documents, reproduced verbatim. Container kernel: the exact three-normal formula for the certified Wetzel triangle. Search family: mirror-symmetric 3-, 5-, and 7-segment polylines.
