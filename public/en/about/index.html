About AMRAL

About AMRAL Research Lab

AMRAL is a replayable research lab for human-led, semi-autonomous, autonomous, and multi-agent mathematical research. It originally grew out of RIITG, RAB, KCPE, and the AMRAL autonomous research loop — now referred to collectively as AMRAL-Core — but the platform itself does not require every research case to use the same methodology. Different cases may adopt AMRAL-Core, direct search, computational exploration, the Triadic Research Protocol (TRP), QCI quantitative closure, formal verification, or other future research methods.

Five Independent Axes

Every research case is described by the following five independent axes, not a single fixed pipeline.

Axis A

Case

What is being studied? The Riemann Hypothesis, the Erdős conjecture series, WOWII graph-theory conjectures, and so on.

Axis B

Methodology

How are research paths generated, expanded, and converged? Could be AMRAL-Core, direct search, a future method, or no fixed methodology at all.

 Axis C

Protocol

How do the AI / research roles collaborate? Currently TRP (Triadic Research Protocol); may be extended in the future.

 Axis D

Autonomy Mode

Who holds the research direction and scheduling? human-led / semi-autonomous / autonomous / multi-agent-autonomous.

 Axis E

Validation

How are research results legitimized, quantitatively closed, and verified? QCI, adversarial review, blind re-derivation, formal proof, and so on.

Name Semantics

AMRAL — the umbrella concept, the Research Lab / Platform, everything on this site.

AMRAL-Core — the original methodology (RIITG + RAB + KCPE + the nine-step research loop), one option under the Methodology axis, not a site-wide mandatory pipeline.

AMRAL Case — an individual research case on the site, e.g. the Riemann Hypothesis, the P/NP duality rehearsal.

The equation AMRAL methodology = everything on this site no longer holds.
