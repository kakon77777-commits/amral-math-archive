NS_GSM: A Canonical Domain Model and Data-Ingestion Specification for the Navier–Stokes Relative-Global Closure Space

The bridge paper where the CSM series stops expanding its abstract theory and, for the first time, fully instantiates it onto the Navier–Stokes long-horizon research corpus, establishing “NS_GSM v0.1.” Fixes three mutually non-collapsing NS domains (formal/Clay-oriented, the signature-parameterized generalized NS-like family, and physical realization); defines the series ontology for nine internal research lines (ETN–X Integration, C1/C2, C3–C6, RFP, MORP, X72, DCRP, FCBP, Proof Asset Map); defines six canonical node families (TARGET, CLAIM, ROUTE, OBSTRUCTION, SURVIVOR, FRONTIER) plus eight support layers; and requires that original source labels (CLOSED/OPEN/NO-GO/SURVIVOR/STOP/CONDITIONAL) pass through a candidate layer and pipeline (Ingest → Extract → Normalize → Validate → ApplyClosure → Rebuild → Snapshot) before they can become native graph state. Defines the seven nodes of the v0.1 seed corpus: ETN–X Integration, C1, C2, C6-Q, DCRP103, DCRP104, DCRP105. The document states explicitly: “NS_GSM” is this series’ canonical project/framework code, and this paper does not presume to supply an English expansion for GSM that its originator never specified.

An original research label must pass through the candidate layer and the six-stage pipeline before it can become native graph state; v0.1 processes only the seven-node seed corpus. §132 explicitly lists eight things the paper does not do, including: it does not perform a full 203+ file ingestion, does not claim route completeness, does not establish an absolute NS proof space, and does not claim any physical-NS modification. Five firewalls (epistemic, mathematical, series, domain, runtime) further lock down the boundary.
