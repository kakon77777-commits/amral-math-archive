NS-DCRP

Navier–Stokes Diffuse Carrier Rigidity Program · Cycle VIII · 55/55 built · global regularity OPEN

NS-DCRP is a continuous research chain advanced round by round through correction: each round directly audits, corrects, or extends the conclusions of the previous one — not a collection of independent topics. The original numbering runs from 01 to 59; this page groups them into seven thematic phases below for easier sequential reading, but the backbone remains a single dependency chain. DCRP is the direct continuation of the previous program, NS-MORP (Minimal Obstruction Rigidity Program, Cycle VII) — MORP-05's final round proved that the ultimate surviving obstruction is a "minimal, zero-tax, kernel-saturated diffuse carrier," and explicitly handed off at its close to "NS-DCRP: Navier–Stokes Diffuse Carrier Rigidity Program" — which is exactly where the name DCRP comes from; the two sub-lines are the two halves of one continuous research line. DCRP itself is Cycle VIII — confirmed later, when building NS-IDRP, from IDRP-01's own opening text ("DCRP Cycle VIII closes with a surviving normal form"), continuing onward through IDRP (IX) → NS-TSKR (X) → NS-RKAP (XI).
