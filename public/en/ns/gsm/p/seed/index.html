NS-GSM · v0.1 Seed Dataset 2026-08-27

The NS_GSM Seed Dataset

Not a paper — a structured dataset (12 YAML files + 5 JSON files, no prose). Extracts 7 logical seed units from 5 real source documents (the ETN-X foundational paper, plus C6-Q, DCRP103, DCRP104, and DCRP105 — C1 and C2 are proof-obligation targets extracted from ETN-X, not separate documents), encoded as a queryable graph: 10 claims, 7 routes, 3 obstructions, 3 survivors, 4 frontiers, 4 bridges, 15 debts, 7 certificates, and 8 nonclaims. Serves as the baseline every later v0.1–v0.7 replay is checked against — validation_report_v0.1.json confirms every parsing/semantic check passed. series.yaml explicitly registers AMRAL's existing NS research lines as series objects, and marks their ingestion status before any document is loaded: ETNX/DCRP as SEED, C as SEED_PARTIAL, X72 as SEED_BRIDGE, and RFP/MORP/FCBP/PAM all PENDING (not yet ingested).

7 logical seed units, with an ingestion-status table for the existing NS research lines; root NS, C1, and C2 are all marked OPEN. The README states plainly that its scope is ‘observed-relative,’ and does not claim route completeness, representation completeness, absolute proof-space coverage, Navier–Stokes global regularity, physical-NS verification, or closure of the generalized NS-like family.
