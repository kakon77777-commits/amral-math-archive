Cycle II, eight rounds · The Eight-Paper Cycle

Dependency chain: spatial synchronization → shell atomization → moving window → escape time → stale floor → preloaded reservoir → closure audit. Reading in order is recommended — each round takes the previous one's results as given.

Spatial Concentration Synchronizer and Wavelength-Cell Dispersion

 CSP-03

Shell Atomization, Spectral-Variance Geometry, and Resonant Transfer

Proves that under severe global shell atomization, the approximate-eigenfunction residual must necessarily grow — a universal residual-gap theorem.

Moving-Window Capture and Dissipation-Wavenumber Geometry

 CSP-05

Escape-Time Synchronization and Temporal-Gap Rigidity

Builds an escape-time calculus for the critical Besov norm, proves every half-layer escape carries a universal recovery-action packet.

 CSP-06

Stale-Floor / Model-Cone Synchronization and Preloaded Reservoir Depth

Proves a model-cone monotonicity principle and a preloaded-reservoir dichotomy; excess preload must force the UV depth past a threshold.

 CSP-07

Preloaded Reservoir Transport and Replenishment Debt

Proves the PRELOAD branch must either survive by exponential amplification or pay a high-frequency Duhamel replenishment debt.

 CSP-08 · Final audit

Unified Reservoir Cover and Cycle-II Closure

Proves the functional-analysis NO-GO, closes with an explicit four-mechanism residual core, hands off to NS-DRC.
