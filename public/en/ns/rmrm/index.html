NS-RMRM

Reverse-engineered Mathematician Research Matrix · Cycle VIII process journal · 1 framework round built · global regularity OPEN

The NS-RMRM source folder holds 54 files structured nothing like the site's other sub-lines: not a batch of independently numbered papers, but 52 versioned checkpoints of a single continuously-growing document (filenames ..._Checkpoint_v3_... through ..._v55_..., missing v15), growing from 61,610 bytes at v3 to 1,509,041 bytes at v55. Reading it confirmed this is not a 12th independent research line — it is the raw research-process journal for NS-DCRP (Cycle VIII) itself. Grepping v55's internal markers line by line — "Checkpoint v2 Update — DCRP-02," "Checkpoint v3 Update — DCRP-03," on through "Checkpoint v55 Update — DCRP-55" — shows the checkpoint version number maps exactly one-to-one to the DCRP round number; a bundled NS_DCRP_37_and_Checkpoint_v37_2026-08-17.zip in the same folder ships the DCRP-37 paper and checkpoint v37 together, the most direct evidence of that correspondence.

v55's own structure explains what it is: Parts I-II are a compressed review of Cycles I-VII (CSP, DRC, ANP, CFOP, FCBP, MORP); Part III defines the RMRM framework itself; from Part IV on, that framework launches a "Composite-Mathematician NS attack" whose output evolves version by version into the formally published NS-DCRP series. In other words, RMRM is the methodology router the researcher actually used to bridge MORP's close and DCRP's opening — DCRP's 55 formal papers are what that routing process produced once filtered, revised, and formalized.

This page therefore builds only one formal entry — RMRM-01, excerpted from Part III of v55, defining the RMRM framework itself (11 cognitive primitives, 38 operators, 28 dynamics, 10 phase-aware mathematician-fingerprint modes, and a research-action value function). This is the one genuinely independent methodological contribution in the whole checkpoint log, distinct from DCRP. The rest of the content — the Cycle review in Parts I-II, and the DCRP attack process from Part IV on — overlaps heavily with the already-built CSP, DRC, ANP, CFOP, FCBP, MORP, and DCRP pages — splitting it into 52 more version-by-version pages would only duplicate content already live, not add anything.

An unexpected find, since fixed: cross-checking checkpoint version numbers against DCRP rounds turned up that NS-DCRP's hub had originally only built rounds 03-55 and 59 (53 papers), on the mistaken assumption that rounds 01-02 existed only as zip archives with no extractable paper. Both release bundles in fact contain a complete paper .md, identical in structure to every round from 03 onward — nobody had actually unzipped them to check. Both rounds have since been built, bringing NS-DCRP to its correct 55 papers (01-55, 59).

A second honest disclosure: Part V of v55 is titled "DCRP-01: Singular-Rooted Compactness Rigidity," but in the formally published NS_DCRP_01_v0.1_release.zip, DCRP-01's actual title is "Carrier Entropy, Rényi Concentration, Nonlinear Concentration Recovery, Source Migration and Atomic Reprofiling Thresholds" — the two differ. This means DCRP-01 was substantially rewritten at least once before its final draft; the process journal preserves an earlier attempt, and the formally published version (built at DCRP-01) is the one that stands. Where the journal and the final draft disagree, this site follows the same rule used everywhere else: the release.zip's formal publication is authoritative.

On the source format, and what was not built version by version

The 52 .md checkpoints in the source folder (v3-v55, missing v15) are all snapshots of one continuously growing document, not 52 independent papers. This English page links through to the Chinese originals for the earliest (v3, 61,610 bytes) and latest (v55, 1,509,041 bytes) raw archives — these are large supplementary process documents, not primary reading content, and have not been translated. The 50 versions in between overlap heavily with those two ends (each version adds to the last rather than replacing it) and are not offered individually; the source folder itself retains the complete 52-version set. Two non-.md files also exist: NS_DCRP_37_and_Checkpoint_v37_2026-08-17.zip is a merged backup of the DCRP-37 paper and checkpoint v37 (identical to the two files that already exist separately); and NS_RMRM_Proof_Process_Checkpoint_2026-08-16.zip is an earlier, unnumbered first checkpoint attempt (21,198 bytes) superseded by the versioned sequence starting at v3. Neither contains content beyond the 52 versions, so neither is included here.
