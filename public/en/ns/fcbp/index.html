NS-FCBP

Navier–Stokes Forest Coercive Budget Program · Cycle VI · 6/6 built · global regularity OPEN

NS-FCBP continues from the forest coercive budget problem left by NS-CFOP Cycle V: can a forest functional be found that is simultaneously globally finite, near-critical on the dangerous cut, and stable under branching/fragmentation? Six rounds carry this forward: FCBP-01 proves the energy-class forcing upper bound and the critical forcing topology differ by exactly one spatial derivative; FCBP-02 tests the filtered vorticity architecture and finds four remaining obstructions; FCBP-03 proves this cycle's first real breakthrough — a non-summable "slow-scale critical lift window"; FCBP-04 closes filter compatibility but reveals a new time-thickness barrier; FCBP-05 proves a sharp half-exponent time threshold; FCBP-06 completes the Cycle VI final audit.

Cycle VI does not produce an unconditional forest coercive budget — FCBP-06 proves the replication-gate NO-GO (inserting the dangerous causal marker itself as an observation coordinate cannot count as a new coercive bridge), a native CAR compiler, and a conditional paid-side absorption compiler, concluding the surviving obstruction is no longer a scale/filter/scheduling construction problem but minimal non-tautological obstruction extraction and rigidity — formally handing off to "NS-MORP: Navier–Stokes Minimal Obstruction Rigidity Program." NS-MORP → NS-DCRP are therefore three consecutive segments of one research line: FCBP (Cycle VI) → MORP (Cycle VII) → DCRP.

Cycle VI, six rounds · The Six-Paper Cycle

Dependency chain: one-derivative gap → filtered coercivity test → slow-scale scheduling breakthrough → moving-filter/horizon alignment → time threshold → causal audit final review. Reading in order is recommended — each round takes the previous one's results as given.

FCBP-01

Critical Forest Coercivity, One-Derivative Gap, and Structural Cancellation

Proves the energy-class forcing upper bound and the critical forcing topology differ by exactly one spatial derivative; defines this cycle's core "critical lift problem."

 FCBP-02

Filtered Stretching Coercivity, Comparable-Annulus Barrier, and Critical-Lift No-Go

Tests whether the filtered vorticity architecture can be upgraded to an unweighted global forest budget — not with existing inequalities alone; reduces to four concrete obstructions.

 FCBP-03 · Breakthrough

Signed Pressure–Flux Work, Slow-Scale Critical Lift, and Filter-Switch Defects

Proves this cycle's first real breakthrough: choosing r_k = r_0(k+1)^(-β) yields a non-summable slow-scale critical lift window.

 FCBP-04

Moving-Filter Telescoping, Horizon Alignment, and Time-Thickness Barrier

Largely closes moving-filter compatibility, but proves a parabolic-alignment summability theorem and a universal time-thickness theorem, revealing a new time barrier.

 FCBP-05

Long-Age Observability and Sharp Half-Exponent Window Threshold

Proves a direct bridge from fresh vorticity to a single work channel is impossible; proves a sharp sequencing theorem for horizon scheduling — exponent 1/2 is the sharp threshold.

 FCBP-06 · Final audit

Causal-to-Audit Transfer, Combined Invisible Cascades, and Cycle-VI Closure Audit

Proves the replication-gate NO-GO and a conditional paid-side absorption compiler. Cycle VI produces no unconditional budget; hands off to NS-MORP.

On the source file format

This page extracts and publishes only each round's core paper .md and the Cycle VI final handoff document; the roadmap/CHANGELOG/schema files inside each bundle are internal project-management documents and are not published.
