# Round 2 Terminology Revision

Round 1 used the term "contact-saturated smooth spiral", but what was actually calibrated at the time was:

\[
\max_s\kappa(s)=\rho^{-1}.
\]

This only indicates that the local radius of curvature reaches the thickness radius:

\[
\min_s\frac{1}{\kappa(s)}=\rho.
\]

It is not equivalent to the global condition:

\[
\operatorname{reach}(\gamma)=\rho.
\]

The global reach is also governed by the closest points between different positions on the curve, intersection of normals, and self-contact.

Therefore, starting from Round 2, it is officially renamed to:

\[
\boxed{\text{curvature-saturated smooth spiral}}
\]

while reserving "contact-saturated" for candidates that truly verify:

\[
\operatorname{reach}(\gamma)=\rho
\]

and only exhibit first boundary contact.
