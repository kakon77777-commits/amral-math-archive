## Quadratic Exponential Limit Family

The radial derivative family in Round 1 is:

\[
f'_{a,c,\varepsilon}(\theta)
=
\exp\left[
-a\exp\left(
-\frac{(\theta-c)^2}{\varepsilon^2}
\right)
\right].
\]

When \(a,\varepsilon\) increase simultaneously, and

\[
q=\frac{a}{\varepsilon^2}
\]

remains finite, after removing the constant factor that will be absorbed by length normalization:

\[
f'_{a,c,\varepsilon}(\theta)
\sim
\exp\left[q(\theta-c)^2\right].
\]

Therefore, we define the stable limit family:

\[
r'(\theta)\propto\exp\left[q(\theta-c)^2\right].
\]

Let:

\[
f(\theta)=\int_0^\theta \exp\left[q(u-c)^2\right]du,
\qquad
r(\theta)=bf(\theta).
\]

The endpoint is determined by:

\[
\Theta+\arctan\frac{f(\Theta)}{f'(\Theta)}=\pi
\]

and the scale \(b\) is determined by \(L=1\).
