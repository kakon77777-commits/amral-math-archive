# Round 3 Method

Taking the positive curvature function

\[
\kappa(s)=\frac{\pi e^{g(s)}}{\int_0^1e^{g(u)}du}
\]

as the primary variable, where \(g\) uses first- and second-order Fourier modes. This parameterization automatically satisfies \(\kappa>0\) and \(\int_0^1\kappa=\pi\). The tangent angle and centerline are then integrated.

The search first uses directional width tension as a proxy, followed by verification using a full translational LP and reflection audit. The common container is constructed by taking the common convex hull of the centerlines, followed by a Minkowski dilation of radius \(\rho\).

The certificate layers are divided into: analytic normal injectivity, outward floating-point curvature boxes, discrete normal intersection replay, end-cap buffer audit, and projected Clarke secant boxes.
