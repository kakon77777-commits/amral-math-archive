## 3. Conclusion

In non-convex containers, the exposure functional possesses a large number of discrete grooves and local branches with respect to rigid body configurations.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Hard cases found by the generator}
\text{ must first survive }
\text{falsification by the placer}.
}
\]

The minimum requirements should include:

1. Both chiralities;
2. Multiple seeds;
3. Separation of low-resolution screening and high-resolution replay;
4. Additional search budget for anomalously high values;
5. Preservation of suboptimal placement branches and convergence gaps.

The truly stable retained candidate in round 10 is not this mixed curve, but the dispersed parent lineage Fourier-22.
