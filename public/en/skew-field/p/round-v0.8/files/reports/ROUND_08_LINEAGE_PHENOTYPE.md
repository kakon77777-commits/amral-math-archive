## 2. Lineage Cannot Predict Phenotype

The Round 9 seed originates from coefficient perturbations of the "dispersed lineage".

However, its final spatial phenotype is derived from:

- Curvature phase;
- Optimal rotation;
- Optimal translation;
- Existing container grooves;

which jointly determine it.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Spectral Lineage}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{Spatial Exposure Phenotype}.
}
\]

Similarly:

\[
\text{Effective Number of Fourier Modes}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{Number of Exposed Components or Gap Entropy}.
\]

## 3. Container Absorption Rate is Also Not a Single-Valued Function of Phenotype

The absorption rate of the Round 7 dispersed type is approximately \(18.3\%\).

Conversely, the absorption rate of the Round 8 localized type is approximately \(34.8\%\).

Therefore, the assumption that:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Localized types are necessarily hard to absorb}
}
\]

is invalid.

The absorption rate is a relational quantity between the attack phenotype and the current container state, rather than an intrinsic property of the curve alone.
