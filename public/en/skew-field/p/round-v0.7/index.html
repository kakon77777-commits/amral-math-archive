The Third Non-Convex Alternating Cycle, Spatial Exposure Entropy, and the Fourier-16 Residual Attack

The third alternation cycle C₆→γ₇→C₇, the first to systematically quantify the “spatial distribution” of exposure rather than just its total amount: this round's attack has fewer effective spectral modes than Round 6's (4.85 versus 6.13), yet the spatial gaps are in fact more scattered (4 connected components, with the largest accounting for only 37%). Proposes the “exposure-mode alternation conjecture”: after the container patches a dispersed-type attack, it may leave a localized-penetration weakness at another scale.
