Round 5 v0.5 2026-07-27

Non-Convex Area-Exposure Tension and the First Curve–Container Alternating Adversarial Cycle

The research question formally shifts: no longer “which curve is hardest to fit inside some convex hull,” but “which curve, even exploiting the container's existing non-convex recesses, still leaves the largest exposed area.” The first complete cycle C₄→γ₅→C₅: the Fourier-12 attack curve exposes 0.00666, container reconfiguration absorbs 57.7%, leaving a net increase of only 1.47%. Held-out sample testing confirms the system has not yet converged.

Finite Fourier family, finite placement search, and non-convex container coordinate-descent candidate — Stage status as stated by the package's own documents, reproduced verbatim. “Genuine universal-container research must be an alternating contest between curvature functions and non-convex containers, not merely ranking curves against a fixed convex container.”
