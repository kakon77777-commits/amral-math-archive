Riemann Hypothesis AI Research Starting Point v1.5: Mixed-Order Cross-Regularity Cancellation

The integration package includes the complete contents of W-11 and W-12—this version builds on the kernel sensitivity discoveries of W-11 to complete W-12. For the first time, an m=1/3 mixed-order kernel dictionary is established, revealing the "cross-regularity cross-cancellation mode": when two kernels of different regularities are superimposed, 93.4% of the self-energy is canceled out, yielding a bracket estimate for λ_min in (1/2000, 1/1000), which has not yet converged to a single exact value.
