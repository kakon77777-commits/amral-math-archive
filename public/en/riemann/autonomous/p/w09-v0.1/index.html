RH-W-09: Adaptive Chamber Continuation

Rewrote the fixed-grid search into a local continuation problem — starting from the W-08 candidate, evaluating nine neighboring points per round, automatically pushing the 15-dimensional lowest generalized spectral bottom all the way down from 1.2×10⁻⁵ to 1.32×10⁻⁹ (a decrease of about 9110 times). The endpoint closely approaches the lag-1 prime activation boundary of log3, differing by only about 9.67×10⁻⁵.

RH_CLAIM = False — The claim status output by the verifier in the package itself, reproduced exactly as is. "15-dimensional positive margin ⇏ RH"; the floating-point continuation path can only output NUMERICAL_CANDIDATE_ONLY, without proving that the spectral bottom achieves a local minimum at this boundary.
