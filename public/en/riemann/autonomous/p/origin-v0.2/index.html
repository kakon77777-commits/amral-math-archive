Riemann Hypothesis AI Research Starting Point v0.2: GAP-ification

Changed from "candidate proof drafts are the unit of research" to "GAP edges G=(A→B) are the unit of research" — establishing 47 first-round GAPs at once, distributed across ten complete routes: Weil-type positivity (W), Nyman–Beurling closure (NB), Li coefficient positivity (LI), Hilbert–Pólya spectral realization (HP), de Bruijn–Newman heat flow (DBN), explicit formulas and prime error (EF), Speiser derivative criterion (SP), random matrix statistics (RMT), Adelic/Connes trace formula (AD), and observation framework and dynamic projection (OD). The subsequent entire W-01~W-20 series is merely the expansion of route W — the GAP Atlas left in this package shows that this is just one of ten parallel routes.

Non-Proof Research Engineering Documents — Self-stated in the "Properties" field of the main document within the package, reproduced as is. "Must not directly demand 'prove RH'"; v0.2 does not use whether RH is proven as the sole criterion for success.
