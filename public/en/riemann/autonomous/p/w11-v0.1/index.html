RH-W-11: Kernel Sensitivity and Regularity Duality

Generalize the single cubic kernel of W-10 to the entire centered cardinal B-spline family, deriving the general prime-power boundary activation law r=m+n+1: the same integer r simultaneously controls boundary regularity, Fourier decay, and tail bound order. The smoother the kernel, the weaker the arithmetic threshold signal, but the easier it is to strictly control the tail bound—there is no single optimal kernel. The 10⁻²⁸ observed in W-10 is the result of the cubic kernel (m=3) suppressing the signal by a factor of 10¹⁶; switching to a linear kernel (m=1) directly amplifies the signal by 1.65×10¹⁶ times.

RH_CLAIM = False — The claim status self-reported by the verifier within the package, reproduced here as is. What is closed in this round is the engineering trade-off of kernel design, not RH positivity; no Weil negative witness was found, nor was any infinite-dimensional positivity proven.
