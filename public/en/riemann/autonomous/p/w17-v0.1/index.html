RH-W-17: Chamber-Aware Slicing and Event Thin Layer

Fixing a 10-dimensional mixed-order Weil dictionary, it establishes the first parameter certificate crossing a spline knot event surface — the three closed intervals of the left chamber, the event thin layer, and the right chamber are all verified to have λmin > 10⁻⁸.

RH_CLAIM = False — The claim status self-reported by the verification script within the package, reproduced here as is. This package neither proves nor disproves the Riemann Hypothesis.
