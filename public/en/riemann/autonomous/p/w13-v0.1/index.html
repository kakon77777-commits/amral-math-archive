RH-W-13: Cross-Regularity Continuation and Canonical Parameters

The explorer once reported a suspected Weil negative direction of -3.32×10⁻⁷ — which, if true, would be a major counterexample. This package neither publicized nor immediately deleted it, but instead upgraded it to a red alert candidate for layer-by-layer investigation according to protocol. The root cause was found to be that the parameters of M were cache-quantized while G was not synchronously quantized, causing an "object identity error" in the generalized eigenvalue problem. After consistent quantization, it flipped positive to +8.76×10⁻⁸, which was confirmed by 80-digit independent integration. Finally, using a jump-resolved tail bound, the exact 10-dimensional mixed generalized spectral bottom was squeezed into (10⁻⁸, 5×10⁻⁸).

RH_CLAIM = False — The claim status output by the verifier within the package itself, reproduced as is. The near-zero positive spectral band is neither an RH proof nor a counterexample; false negatives have been explicitly marked as NUMERICAL_FALSE_NEGATIVE_REJECTED, not quietly removed.
