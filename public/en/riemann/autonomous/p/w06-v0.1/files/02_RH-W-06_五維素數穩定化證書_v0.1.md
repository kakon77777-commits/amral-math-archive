# 1. Certificate Objective

The matrix elements are not single numbers, but rational intervals:

$$
M_{ij}\in[\underline M_{ij},\overline M_{ij}].
$$

We want to prove:

$$
\boxed{
\text{All real symmetric matrices conforming to these intervals are positive definite}
}.
$$

This is stronger than proving that a specific floating-point midpoint matrix is positive definite, and it avoids hiding numerical errors within eigenvalue computations.
