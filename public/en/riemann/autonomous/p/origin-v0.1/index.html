Riemann Hypothesis AI Research Starting Point v0.1

The true starting point of the entire research line — a line-by-line cleanup of four old manuscripts written by Neo.K (two versions of "Riemann Hypothesis Thought Experiment 3.0", "Observer Dimensionality Theory of Analytic Continuation", and "Dynamic Projection Experiment"): categorizing them into "known classical results", "reproducible numerical calculations", "valuable research intuitions", and "overclaims and circular reasoning", explicitly listing the author's own past reasoning errors (the unique optimal observation angle, scanning β to find β=1/2, coordinate distortion generating new information, etc.) as content that must be deleted or downgraded.

Non-proof, non-theorem declaration, non-claim of the Riemann Hypothesis being true — The "Explicit Status" field at the beginning of the main document in the package self-reports, reproduced here as-is: "This document does only four things: preserves the original intent of the research, organizes known and verifiable mathematical anchor points, downgrades unestablished statements into falsifiable research hypotheses, and establishes a research state that can be jointly advanced by AI and computation."
