# Cleanup Comparison of Four Sources

**Date:** July 23, 2026  
**Purpose:** To record which contents are retained, demoted, corrected, or removed, preventing future versions from reintroducing known errors.

---

## 1. "RH3.0 Public Corrected Version"

### Retained

- The Euler product converges directly only for $\operatorname{Re}(s)>1$;
- The completed $\xi$ function and the functional equation;
- Symmetric orbits of the zeros;
- The de Bruijn–Newman framework;
- The argument principle;
- The non-multiplicativity of logarithmic Gaussian weights;
- Records of breakpoints in failed proofs;
- The research ethic that "failures must leave precise coordinates."

### Removed

- The original RH proof chain;
- Berry phase cancellation;
- The assertion that losing the Euler product after deformation implies a contradiction;
- The argument that the normal functioning of technology supports RH;
- Undefined instantaneous deformation operators.

## 2. "RH3.0 Public Final Version"

### Retained

- The method of separating the original chain from the corrected chain;
- Reproducible calculations of theta, Mellin, argument, and weights;
- The correct signs for the DBN backward heat equation;
- The separation of algorithmic value from RH proof value;
- The original intention in the philosophical conclusion: honestly recording the cliffs and bringing back usable tools.

### Demoted

- "Algorithmic value" is changed to the reproduction and assembly of classical tools, not listed as a new invention;
- High-precision floating-point results are relabeled as `REPRODUCED`, not as rigorous certificates;
- Only interval arithmetic with complete error bounds can be labeled as `CERTIFIED_NUMERICAL`.

## 3. "Observer Dimensionality Theory of Analytic Continuation"

### Retained Original Intentions

- Different representations may expose different structures;
- One should clarify the framework being used before conducting research;
- Searching for cross-representation invariants;
- Transforming the choice of representation into a comparable parametric problem;
- Exploring the possible formalization of "families of representations" using the languages of fibers, categories, and gauges.

### Demoted to Research Hypotheses

- High-dimensional ontology and low-dimensional projections;
- Families of observational frameworks;
- Task-relative optimal representations;
- Describing representation transformations via fiber bundles/groupoids/categories;
- Analogies with Yoneda, gauge theory, and quantum representations.

### Removed

- "The relativistic revolution of mathematics";
- "Discoveries beyond the Riemann Hypothesis";
- "All mathematical objects are projections" as an axiom;
- "Every object has a unique optimal angle";
- "The Cauchy–Riemann equations are the optimal projection conditions";
- "Holomorphy is distortion-free" as a mathematical equality;
- "Riemann zeros are local sections";
- "Zeta may be multi-valued under different analytic continuation paths";
- Unbridged extrapolations to Navier–Stokes, algebraic geometry, topology, and category theory;
- Claims of historical status, schools of thought, and revolutions.

## 4. "Dynamic Projection Experiments"

### Retained

- The experimental spirit of making representations or deformations into adjustable parameters;
- Zero tracking, sensitivity, spectral statistics, and visualization;
- Doing control groups first, then non-trivial deformations;
- Using numerical experiments to discover candidate propositions;
- "Play first, rigorize later" as a creative research workflow.

### Technical Corrections

1. `twisted_zeta(s)=zeta(T_alpha(s))` is a pre-composition transformation, not a new analytic continuation.
2. If $T_\alpha$ is invertible, the zeros are merely coordinate shifts under $T_\alpha^{-1}$.
3. `zetazero(n)` already uses zeros on the critical line and cannot be used again to prove the critical line is optimal.
4. The raw spacing coefficient of variation is unsuitable as an indicator of truth; the mean density should be unfolded first.
5. Undefined functions like `find_zeros_with_real_part(beta)` and `measure_perfection` cannot constitute runnable experiments.
6. Arbitrarily weighted "perfection degrees" easily hardcode the expected answer into the objective function.
7. Numerical optima can only be labeled as experimental results, not as numerical proofs.
8. Searching along different $\beta$ lines cannot exclude zeros in unsearched regions.

### Removed

- Fabricated numerical results;
- The claim that $\alpha=0$ must be the minimum;
- The assertion that a peak at $\beta=\tfrac12$ proves RH;
- "Irrefutable numerical results";
- The narrative of publishing experimental verification of RH within three months;
- Any statements replacing logical implication with visual clarity.

# 5. Anti-Regression Rules for Future Versions

If the following phrases appear in new drafts, an audit must be automatically triggered:

- "Therefore, RH holds";
- "Numerical proof";
- "Unique optimal angle";
- "Symmetry forces every zero to be fixed";
- "Not multiplicative, hence a contradiction";
- "Analytic continuation has multiple arbitrary parameter versions";
- "The image is more regular, so the proposition is true";
- "Formalization complete", but `sorry` or main bridge hypotheses still exist;
- "Verified", but without an error model, interval certificates, or reproducible code.

All such phrases must be rewritten as:

- Unproven hypotheses;
- Finite numerical observations;
- Task-relative optimization results;
- Citations of known results;
- Or explicit `BLOCKED` / `REFUTED` statuses.
