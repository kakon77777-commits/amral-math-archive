RH-W-19: Reproducibility and Adversarial Certificate Audit

Placing the W-18 unified backend into a replayable attack model — establishing a zoo of 17 classes of false certificates (Z01–Z17). 16 classes of attacks that should be rejected were all correctly rejected. The 17th class, "simultaneous collusion of verifier and artifact," is deliberately marked as EXPECTED_SURVIVAL: a self-certifying system itself cannot rule out this kind of attack and must introduce an external root of trust. Accompanied by a concrete empirical proof — a 14th-order Hilbert matrix is proven positive-definite using exact rational LDLT, yet the double-precision eigenvalue solver in this environment returns a negative minimum eigenvalue.

RH_CLAIM = False — The claim status self-reported by the verifier within the package, reproduced here as is. "RH-W-19 establishes a 'certificate of certificate failure'. It does not elevate any RH truth value claims, but only improves the identifiability of research artifacts against errors, tampering, and overclaims."
