# RH-W-08: Archimedean Tail Bound Refinement
## From Absolute Value Majorants to Sign-Preserving Derivative Tail Sums

**Version:** v0.1  
**Date:** 2026-07-23  
**Status:** `CLOSED_FOR_COMPACT_BETA7_CORRELATIONS`

# 1. Origin of the Problem

The even part of the dual correlation function is denoted as:

$$
F(x)=f(x)+f(-x),
$$

and:

$$
I(a)=\int_0^r F(x)e^{-ax}\,dx.
$$

The Archimedean term requires handling:

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}
\left[
I(a_k)-\frac{F(0)}{b_k}
\right],
$$

where:

$$
a_k=2k+\frac12,
\qquad
b_k=2k+1.
$$

In the previous version, after finite truncation, all higher-order tail terms were bounded by symmetric absolute value intervals. This approach is safe, but it loses the known signs of $F''(0)$ and $F^{(4)}(0)$, causing the tail bound width to remain at the $O(K^{-2})$ scale.

When the lowest eigenvalue falls to the $10^{-5}$ scale or smaller, this error becomes unacceptable.

---

# 2. Seven-fold Integration by Parts

For the $\beta_7$ correlation function in this iteration, $F$ is a compactly supported, piecewise degree-seven polynomial with $C^6$ regularity.

Since $F$ is an even function:

$$
F'(0)=F^{(3)}(0)=F^{(5)}(0)=0.
$$

The zero at the right endpoint of the support has sufficient multiplicity, so integration by parts yields:

$$
I(a)
=
\frac{F_0}{a}
+
\frac{F_2}{a^3}
+
\frac{F_4}{a^5}
+
\frac{F_6}{a^7}
+
R_8(a),
$$

where:

$$
F_{2j}=F^{(2j)}(0),
$$

and:

$$
\boxed{
|R_8(a)|
\le
\frac{B_7}{a^8}
}
$$

where $B_7$ is the maximum absolute value of the seventh derivative across all polynomial segments.

Since the seventh derivative is constant on each segment, $B_7$ can be obtained exactly from the rational polynomials.

---

# 3. Closed Form Still Used for the Leading Tail

Define:

$$
S_0(K)
=
\sum_{k=K}^{\infty}
\left(
\frac1{a_k}-\frac1{b_k}
\right).
$$

The complete sum is:

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}
\left(
\frac1{2k+1/2}-\frac1{2k+1}
\right)
=
\frac\pi4+\frac{\log2}{2}.
$$

Therefore:

$$
S_0(K)
=
\frac\pi4+\frac{\log2}{2}
-
\sum_{k=0}^{K-1}
\left(
\frac1{a_k}-\frac1{b_k}
\right).
$$

$\pi$ and $\log2$ are bounded by Machin-type arctangent series and rational atanh series, respectively.

---

# 4. Preserving the Signs of $F_2, F_4, F_6$

For:

$$
S_p(K)
=
\sum_{k=K}^{\infty}a_k^{-p},
\qquad p>1,
$$

the function:

$$
x\longmapsto(2x+1/2)^{-p}
$$

is monotonically decreasing. Thus:

$$
\int_K^{\infty}(2x+1/2)^{-p}\,dx
\le
S_p(K)
$$

and:

$$
S_p(K)
\le
(2K+1/2)^{-p}
+
\int_K^{\infty}(2x+1/2)^{-p}\,dx.
$$

The integral is an exact rational number:

$$
\boxed{
\int_K^{\infty}(2x+1/2)^{-p}\,dx
=
\frac{(2K+1/2)^{1-p}}{2(p-1)}.
}
$$

Therefore, $S_3, S_5, S_7, S_8$ can all be handled using purely rational upper and lower bounds.

The new tail interval is:

$$
\boxed{
F_0S_0(K)
+
F_2S_3(K)
+
F_4S_5(K)
+
F_6S_7(K)
+
[-B_7S_8^+(K),B_7S_8^+(K)]
}.
$$

The key difference is that:

$$
F_2S_3,
\quad
F_4S_5,
\quad
F_6S_7
$$

are no longer rewritten as absolute value intervals centered at zero.

---

# 5. Change in the Order of Error

The previous version used the following for $F_2S_3$:

$$
[-|F_2|U_3,|F_2|U_3],
$$

whose interval width is of the same order as $S_3$ itself:

$$
O(K^{-2}).
$$

The new version is only responsible for the difference between the upper and lower bounds of $S_3$. From the monotonic integral bounds, this difference is at most the first term:

$$
(2K+1/2)^{-3}=O(K^{-3}).
$$

Similarly:

- the uncertainty width of $F_4S_5$ is $O(K^{-5})$;
- the uncertainty width of $F_6S_7$ is $O(K^{-7})$;
- the sum of the remainder terms is $O(K^{-7})$.

Therefore, the dominant error improves from:

$$
O(K^{-2})
$$

to:

$$
\boxed{O(K^{-3})}.
$$

# 7. Trust Boundary

`arch_tail_refinement.py` is responsible for generating rational intervals, and its proof relies on:

1. The piecewise rational polynomial representation of $\beta_7$;
2. $C^6$ smoothness;
3. Seven-fold integration by parts;
4. Integral upper and lower bounds for monotonically decreasing series;
5. Existing interval backends for $\pi$, $\log2$, and the exponential function.

`verify_refined_certificate.py` does not recompute these analytical steps. It only verifies:

- The provided matrix intervals;
- The exact Gram matrix;
- The row-radius;
- The rational $LDL^T$ positive definiteness certificate.

Therefore, the current tag remains:

```text
RIGOROUS_NUMERICAL_CERTIFICATE
UNDER_DOCUMENTED_SOFTWARE_CONTRACT
```

rather than a fully formalized theorem.

# 8. Generalizability and Limitations

This set of tail bounds can be directly generalized to:

- Other translations;
- Other rational scales $h$;
- Any finite number of $\beta_7$ correlation matrix elements.

If a higher-degree B-spline is used instead, more derivative terms can be preserved, allowing the order of the tail bound to further increase; however, the following must be re-established:

- Regularity order;
- Boundary vanishing order;
- Derivative sup bounds;
- Polynomial segment verification.

Thus, it is not a black-box tail bound for all test functions, but rather a verifiable compilation rule within the dictionary of this project.
