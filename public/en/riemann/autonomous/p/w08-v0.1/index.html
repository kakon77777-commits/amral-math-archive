RH-W-08: Chamber Search and Strict Refinement

After scanning 122 translated B-spline chambers on a fixed grid and selecting the top candidate (h=3/20, d=9/40, N=13), the first rigorous reconstruction yielded only INCONCLUSIVE because the old Archimedean tail bound was too loose — this round switched to a tail bound that preserves the derivative signs of F₂/F₄/F₆, improving the certificate resolution by 63 times and successfully proving a 13-dimensional generalized positive margin of 10⁻⁵.

RH_CLAIM = False — The declared status output by the verifier itself in the package, reproduced here as-is. "13-dimensional strict positivity ⇏ RH"; the search layer can only output NUMERICAL_CANDIDATE, and only the interval generator and exact verifier can determine the certificate status.
