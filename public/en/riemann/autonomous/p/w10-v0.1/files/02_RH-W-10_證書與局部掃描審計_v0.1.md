Even if the locally added block is extremely small, changing $d$ simultaneously changes:

- The correlation centers of all lags;
- The Gram matrix;
- The endpoint terms;
- The Archimedean terms;
- Other activated prime-power sample values.

Therefore, one cannot replace the complete post-boundary matrix with "just adding a $P_3$". `theta_plus_15x15_interval.json` is the reassembled complete explicit formula certificate.

## 4. Software Trust Boundary

The rigorous path does not use binary floating-point numbers. Transcendental quantities are handled via:

- $\log n$: Rational atanh series;
- $\pi$: Machin's formula;
- $\sqrt n$: Integer square root enclosure;
- Euler's constant: Digamma/Binet signed remainders;
- $e^x$: Documented correctly rounded `Decimal.exp()` followed by outward expansion.

The tags remain:

```text

UNDER_DOCUMENTED_SOFTWARE_CONTRACT
```

rather than a formalized theorem.
