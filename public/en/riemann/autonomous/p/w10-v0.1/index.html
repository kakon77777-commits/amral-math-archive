RH-W-10: Prime Boundary Local Modes and Seventh-Order Soft Start

Precisely derived the soft-start law of prime-3 at the log3=d+4h support boundary — new matrix elements soft-start with (μ₊)⁷, with regularity exactly C⁶ but not C⁷. This overturns the intuition that "the matrix jumps sharply as soon as a prime enters," and proves that the direct magnitude upon entry (about 10⁻²⁸) is far smaller than the current near-zero spectral bottom of 10⁻⁹. Therefore, the searcher approaching the boundary is a genuine geometric phenomenon, not caused by this entry event.

RH_CLAIM = False — The claim status output by the verifier within the package itself, reproduced exactly as is. Both complete fifteen-dimensional chambers before and after the boundary (θ₋, θ₊) are proven by pure rationals to have a positive margin >10⁻⁹; local scanning did not observe an avoided crossing, but this is a numerical diagnostic, not a full-parameter interval theorem.
