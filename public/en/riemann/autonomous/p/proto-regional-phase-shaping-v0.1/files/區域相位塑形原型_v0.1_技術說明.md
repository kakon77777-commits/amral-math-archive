# Regional Phase Shaping Prototype v0.1

This engineering package implements the first computable arrow proposed in the previous paper:

\[
\text{Off-axis spectral rectangle}
\longrightarrow
\text{Real even compactly supported test function}
\longrightarrow
\text{Regional negative orbit block}.
\]

The prototype does not read any unknown ζ zero positions; it only accepts the rectangle geometry. It constructs in a real even pairwise bump basis

\[
\psi(t)=\sum_kc_k\phi_k(t),
\]

such that

\[
G(w)=\widehat\psi(w)
\]

approximates \(i\) on the rectangle, and precisely enforces the floating-point linear conditions via null space projection:

\[
G(i/2)=G(-i/2)=0.
\]

The output includes grid negative values, endpoint residuals, symmetry residuals, preimage samples, and candidate continuous upper bounds.

## Success Criteria

The minimum success criterion for the numerical exploration phase is:

\[
\max_{w\in K_{\rm grid}}
2\operatorname{Re}(G(w)^2)<0.
\]

A stronger criterion is:

\[
B_{\max}^{\rm grid}+L_Br<0,
\]

where \(L_B\) is the Lipschitz upper bound of the block. This upper bound in v0.1 is not yet interval-strict.

## Research Status

Even if the prototype succeeds, it only implies that all hypothetical off-axis zeros within the rectangle will form a negative contribution. It does not mean that the total Weil quadratic form is negative, because there are still:

- Positive squared moduli of zeros on the critical line;
- Other off-axis orbits;
- Archimedean terms;
- Prime and prime power terms;
- The tail at infinity.

The next engineering package will convert the same basis into an arithmetic matrix to test whether the local negative direction overlaps with the provably positive semi-definite cone.
