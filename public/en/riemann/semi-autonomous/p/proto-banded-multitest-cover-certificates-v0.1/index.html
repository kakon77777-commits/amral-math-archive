Banded Multi-Test Functions and Adaptive Cover Certificate Families

Banded Multi-Test Cover Certificates v0.1 — Replaces "one function, one rectangle" with an adaptive anisotropic cover of 18 rational rectangles, reducing the axis energy by about 215 times, but the global remainder under unit negative margin still falls entirely into negative values.
