## Execution Sequence

### 1. Source lock

Establish a theorem registry, containing at least:

- Zero count / argument-principle or Turing-style sources;
- Multiplicity and endpoint conventions;
- Tail bounds;
- The version of the explicit formula used;
- Validity ranges;
- Exact sources for all constants;
- Source file or URL hash.

Arbitrarily filling in constants from summary texts is not allowed.

### 2. Conditional occupancy

Prove a conditional interface:

$$
\exists\ \rho\ \text{off-axis zero}
\Longrightarrow
\exists\ \text{rational cell }C
$$

where $C$ possesses presence, multiplicity, boundary, and symmetry data available for operator transfer.

The zero location remains a quantified variable. Do not refer to the $58$ dual atoms of v0.7 or the height-$20.4$ prototype as actual zeros.

### 3. Explicit-formula transfer

For the same clamped test-function object, complete:

- Analytic admissibility;
- Density and limit exchange;
- Zero/tail convergence;
- Legal orientation of the five bands and tail coefficients;
- Directed interval bounds;
- Prime-side expression.

If legally oriented coefficients invalidate the v0.7 witness, optimization should be redone; restoring the known erroneous upper-to-lower transfer is strictly prohibited.

### 4. Compose one parameterized cell

Compose the conditional occupancy with the interval Green family. A successful output can only be:

- A machine-checkable parameterized conditional cell theorem; or
- A formal robust-failure/no-go record.

Merely enlarging $h$ further from $1.78\times10^{-6}$ does not count as closing the main GAP.

### 5. Only then consider global scaling

Only after the aforementioned cell theorem is established should one address full height, full off-axis regions, unknown leakage, tail, and prime-side exhaustion.
