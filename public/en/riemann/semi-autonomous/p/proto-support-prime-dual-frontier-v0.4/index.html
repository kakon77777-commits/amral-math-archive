Support–Prime Dual Frontier

Support–Prime Dual Frontier v0.4 — Expands the dual witness method to four support radii, all of which are blocked by safe lower bounds (2.62 → 1.09), while catching a methodological trap: coarse axis grids generate false escapes. The prime enumeration cost at R=16 has reached 79 trillion terms.

RH_CLAIM = False — The files in the package self-report "not a proof of RH, not a disproof of RH, nor an interval certificate for the continuous analytic problem", reproduced here as-is. The safe lower bounds for all four radii are strictly greater than 1, and all 12 witnesses are floating-reconstructed and remain PSD.
