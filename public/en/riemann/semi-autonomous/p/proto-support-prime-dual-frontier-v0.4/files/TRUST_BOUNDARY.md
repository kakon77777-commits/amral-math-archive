$$
W=T+\sum_j\underline N_j\int P\,d\mu_j
+\alpha\int C\,d\nu\succeq0,
$$

then for any specified discrete primal-feasible $A\succeq0$,

$$
J(A)\ge\alpha.
$$

Furthermore,

$$
\operatorname{supp}\psi\subset[-R,R]
\Longrightarrow
p^m<e^{2R}
$$

is the exact support cutoff of this construction.

## Key Interpretations

`at_least_one_searched_patch_blocked=true` only indicates that: given the specified radius,
dictionary, discrete axis grid, core points, and floating matrices, at least one searched
sub-rectangle cannot possibly achieve $J(A)<1$.

`full_refined_cover_joint_gate_exhausted=false` must remain visible. It indicates that this node
did not perform joint optimization on the 288 sub-rectangles one by one.

An $\alpha<1$ on a coarse grid cannot be interpreted as primal feasible; whereas a reconstructed and PSD
$\alpha>1$ is sufficient to reject the corresponding finite primal branch.
