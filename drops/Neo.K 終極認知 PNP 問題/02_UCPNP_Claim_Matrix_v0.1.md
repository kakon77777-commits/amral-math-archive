## Explicit non-claims

1. The paper does not prove classical $P=NP$ or $P\neq NP$.
2. The six phases are not claimed to be neurobiologically fundamental.
3. The $3\times3$ and $6\times3$ matrices are semantic/accounting interfaces, not universal empirical laws.
4. SDPE-BEB is used only as a controlled micro-model, not as proof of universal cognition.
5. No statement about ASI is elevated above empirical/conjectural status in this paper.
