## Explicit non-claims

Paper 03 does **not** claim that:

1. $\Sigma$ or $\Gamma$ is a discovered neural or physical scalar;
2. human cognition is completely described by these two axes;
3. exact state abstraction is identical to general dimension generation;
4. SDPE-BEB proxy values generalize to AGI/ASI;
5. faster computation implies larger $\Sigma$ or $\Gamma$;
6. larger $\Sigma$ or $\Gamma$ implies larger dignity, moral status, or ontological rank;
7. UCPNP resolves classical $P$ versus $NP$.
