# 3. Theorem — rigidity of state-preserving affine normalization

$$

$$

\boxed{

\qquad

\tag{3.1}

### Proof

$$

=
AC
(\partial_tu)(Bx,Ct),

$$

=

=
A^2B

Since

$$

$$

$$

Finally,

$$

=

$$

$$

Therefore the transformation is exactly

$$

=

$$

\square
